Thoughtful by design

The small things
add up.

Auralis is a launcher, not another streaming service. It helps you get to what’s already on your TV, then adds a few useful ways to browse and keep track.

Your apps, ready when you are.

Auralis discovers launchable apps on your TV, puts them in one place, and keeps a short list of recently opened apps for quick launch.

After a stretch without interaction, the ambient screen can show the time over cinematic artwork. You can also start it from Control Center and return with the remote.

Artwork availability depends on the device’s network connection and image cache.

Clear about what it is

A better front door.
Your apps stay in charge.

Auralis does not stream films or TV. It helps you discover a title, then opens a trailer, IMDb details, or a search on an installed service when Android can handle that link.

01

No universal playback progress. Android does not give Auralis a shared Continue Watching feed from other apps. “Recently opened” is launcher history, not viewing progress.

02

Your list is local. My List saves catalogue picks on this device, per Auralis profile. It does not sync across TVs or streaming accounts.

03

Discovery is a starting point. The current film shelf is a limited external catalogue. A service search does not guarantee availability, subscription access, or playback.

04

Your TV makes the final call. Default launcher selection depends on Android version and manufacturer settings. Auralis can request the Home role where the device supports it.
